Who We Are

Built for the Love of Making

Artisan Crafts was born in 2016 out of frustration with mass production. Our founder, Emily Ross, a ceramicist herself, wanted a platform that put the maker first — where every product had a story, a hand, and a purpose behind it.

Today, we work with over 400 independent artisans across 30 countries, offering small-batch, ethically made products to customers who care about where things come from.

Our Mission

Craft with a Conscience

We believe that how something is made matters as much as what it is. Every product on our platform is made in limited batches by craftspeople who are paid fairly and given the time to work with care.

We're committed to keeping traditional techniques alive and ensuring that the artisans behind each piece are the ones who benefit most from the sale.

The People Behind It

Meet Our Artisans

Every product tells the story of a skilled maker.

Maria Santos

Ceramicist · Portugal

Maria has been throwing pots for 22 years. Her ceramic tea sets are made using traditional techniques passed down from her grandmother.

Rupa Sharma

Weaver · Rajasthan, India

Using handlooms that have been in her family for three generations, Rupa creates woollen pieces that take up to a week each to complete.

Tomás Ferreira

Silversmith · Mexico City

A self-taught jeweller who learned his craft through apprenticeship in Taxco, Mexico's silver capital. Each piece is hammered and set by hand.
