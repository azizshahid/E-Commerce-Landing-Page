Each tea set is wheel-thrown and hand-painted in Maria's small studio in Porto, Portugal. The glaze is fired at 1260°C, giving it a distinctive matte surface that is both food-safe and dishwasher-safe. No two sets are identical — minor variations in colour and texture are an intentional feature of the handmade process.

The 4-piece set includes a teapot (750ml), two mugs (280ml each), and a small tray. Each piece is signed and dated by Maria on the base.

The Maker

Maria Santos

Ceramicist  ·  Porto, Portugal  ·  22 years experience

Maria has been throwing pots since she was fourteen, learning at her grandmother's wheel in the hills outside Porto. Her ceramic tea sets take up to three days each to complete — thrown, trimmed, bisque fired, hand-painted, then high-fired. She works in limited batches of six at a time, and every piece is signed.
